Case studies

Selected work, 2016 to 2026

Patter AI · 2023 – 2026

Problem

Setting up a client took 2 weeks. Configuring a product took 10 minutes. All manual.

Solution

AI agents do the reading. A person checks every result.

Impact

Client setup2 weeks → 48 hours

Product setup10 min → 2 min

Adding a lender, excluding compliance review4 weeks → 1 week

Manual utility-data work7-person team → 1 expert reviewer

My roleLed product at a 12-person company with 4 engineers. Decided how much each agent could do before a person had to approve, wrote the regression scenarios, and gated every release on them.

ADT Solar · 2020 – 2023 · 35,000+ lender credit applications a month

Problem

Reps drew their own roof designs, and 80% of those led to costly changes after the sale. For professionally drawn designs it was 14%.

Solution

Used that gap to help justify professional design, then put AI design inside the sales tool reps already used.

Impact

Drawing a design15 min → 30 sec

New-rep training6 weeks → 2 weeks

My roleFirst product manager for ADT’s solar division. Grew product from a CTO and 2 contract engineers to 3 scrum teams, hired 2 PMs, and rolled releases out one territory at a time.

Palmetto · 2016 – 2019

Problem

A solar proposal took about an hour.

Solution

Digital pricing, then one platform to design, price and check credit.

Impact

Building a proposalAbout 1 hour → minutes

Lead marketplace launched11,500+ consultations a month

My roleStarted as a solar engineer, became Palmetto’s first product manager, then Director of Product for Alchemy, its point-of-sale platform. Led a 25-person external development team in India and launched the lead marketplace.
