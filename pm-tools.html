How I work

A product studio of one, run like a team.

I have about an hour a night. These are the tools and rituals that let me ship several products at once without losing the thread. The two apps below are live demos with my real slate and roadmap as of October 2026; anything you change stays in your browser.

Build Slate

One board for every product: stage (idea, spec, building, beta, launched, paused), the very next step, and a milestone date. It caps me at five live projects so I go deep, not wide.

Try Build Slate

Near-term roadmap

Now, Next, Later and Done lanes with a board per product, owners, session-size estimates and a blocked flag. Any AI session can read and update it by link, so the roadmap stays current without me retyping it.

Try the roadmap

Source for both apps, plus the PM templates and routines below, is on GitHub.

Decisions that leave a trail

  • Decision log: every call records why, and what it beat.
  • Persona check: before a product decision is final, each persona gets a green or red with a one-line reason. CI fails if a decision file skips it.
  • Anchors: a few principles every feature is checked against, like "anxiety-less" and "stays on US soil."
  • Post mortems: each one adds a rule, so the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

An AI team with lanes

Specialist agents each own one job: staff PM, QA (tests first), engineer, privacy and security, UX for low-tech users, and marketing. I keep an inventory of every agent's maturity (proven, working, dormant or broken) and decide which to use, mature or retire.

One hub across projects

Each project reports into a central product office: products, PM tools, agents, decisions and open loops, so nothing lives only in one chat.
