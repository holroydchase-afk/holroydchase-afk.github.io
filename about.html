About

From rooftops to roadmaps.

I started out designing solar systems and ended up building the software that prices, sells and finances them. Pricing, payments and point-of-sale lending are my home turf, and I design my own screens and prototype with the newest AI.

The path

  1. Solar engineer, Palmetto (2016). Designed 2,000+ residential and commercial PV systems and replaced 75% of on-site surveys with virtual ones, saving $80K a year.
  2. First PM, then Senior PM, then Director of Product, Palmetto (2017–2019). Launched the sales enablement platform, built Alchemy (design, point of sale and project management with real-time credit decisions), and launched a machine-learning lead marketplace supporting 11,500+ consultations a month.
  3. Founder, Bigfoot Energy (2019–2020). A smart-home and clean-energy company in Charleston, WV.
  4. Product Owner, AGDATA (2020). Scoped a master data platform to replace 8+ legacy pricing and rebate systems.
  5. PM, then Senior PM, ADT (2020–2023). First PM for a 3,000-person solar division. Built the credit-check and offer engine (12,000+ applications a month), a lender allocation platform that helped cut dealer fees 3–5 points, a channel-partner pricing platform, and the Lowe's in-store program. Shipped to about 5,000 reps.
  6. Staff PM, Patter AI (2023–2026). A real-time pricing engine across loans, leases and PPAs, rep-guided checkout, AI conversation analysis that found 40% of a client's reps going off-script, and a rules engine that cut lender onboarding from about a month to a week.
  7. Since Jan 2026: independent product work. Between roles, I'm building AI-agent tools in public (job search, AI energy, PM operations) to stay sharp on how LLM products ship. Not raising, not selling; ready for a full-time role.

What I care about

  • Checkout, pricing and payments that feel simple to the person at the kitchen table.
  • Clean energy and climate, rooted in a WVU degree in Sustainable Design.
  • Privacy that's strong underneath and obvious on the surface.
  • Calm teams that ship.
